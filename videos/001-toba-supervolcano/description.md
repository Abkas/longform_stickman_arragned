## Description (paste as-is into the YouTube description box)

```
74,000 years ago, the Toba supervolcano erupted in Sumatra — the largest volcanic event of the last 2 million years. Ash from it has been found 9,000 km away, buried in the exact dirt layer where people were living on the South African coast at the time.

The story you've probably heard is that it nearly wiped out the entire human species — down to a few thousand survivors, worldwide. It's in documentaries, textbooks, probably a very confident tweet.

When scientists actually checked, none of it happened. This video covers what the real evidence — from the same sites, the same ash layer — actually shows: not a species clinging to survival, but one of the most technologically and socially advanced stretches of early human behavior on record, happening right through the eruption.
```
