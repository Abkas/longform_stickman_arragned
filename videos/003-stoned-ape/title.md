## Topic

Terence McKenna's 1992 "Stoned Ape Hypothesis" — psilocybin mushrooms as an
accidental evolutionary catalyst for human language and consciousness.
Genuinely controversial, mostly-rejected-by-science claim, presented
honestly (not debunked as a gimmick, not endorsed uncritically): the actual
argument, the disputed "evidence" people cite for it (Tassili n'Ajjer rock
art), the undisputed evidence that has nothing to do with the evolution
claim (Kaminaljuyú mushroom stones, ~1000 BCE), and the reframe — the
theory is wrong, but the underlying human relationship with this substance
across multiple independent cultures is real, ancient, and arguably more
interesting than the myth.
