# Video 003 — shot list

Status: **locked, built 2026-08-23 on a verified programmatic basis — Shot
density v2 (maximum density + itemized-list override), per
`system/04_shots/shot.md`, used from video 003 onward.** Machine-parseable
by `system/05_visuals/generate_visuals.py` — keep the `## Shot NNN` headers
and `- Narration:`/`- Duration:`/`- Type:`/`- Visual:`/`- Prompt:` field
labels exactly as formatted.

**How this was built:** `script.md`'s locked narration (2454 clean
words, tags converted, italics markdown stripped) was split programmatically
into its 89 real sentences, then further split at clause boundaries
(commas, semicolons, em-dashes, coordinating/subordinating conjunctions) per
v2's higher-density rule. Fragments under 4 words were merged forward into
the next segment (v2's revised merge threshold, down from v1's 8-word
threshold), except for segments belonging to a detected itemized list
(comma/`or`/`and`-separated enumerations of 2+ concrete items), which are
exempt from merging and instead get their own per-item shot regardless of
word count, per the itemized-list override. This produced **262
shots** — 69 `character`, 145 `diagram`, 48 `environment` —
of which **38 are list/"flash" item-shots** (`~1.5-2.5s` each,
several run back-to-back as quick-cut bursts) across 12 detected
rule-of-three/rule-of-four enumerations spread through all five pillars.
10 shots are marked `- Type: video` (see the build report for the
full list and reasoning) — flagged generously per the pacing-fix section's
"raise the video ratio" direction, not a hard percentage target.

Total computed duration: **722s (~12.04 min)**
against `script.md`'s own stated target of **~11.8 min**. This is an
approximation, same caveat as every prior video's shot list
(`system/07_assembly/assembly.md`'s "Known limitation" — no real per-word
narration timestamps exist yet, so duration here is `words / 207 WPM` for
narrative shots and a fixed `~1.5-2.5s` flash tier for list-item shots,
not measured against real audio).

Narration verified to reconstruct `script.md`'s narration text word-for-word,
in order, with no gaps or overlaps (checked programmatically, not by eye).

**Color palette (locked for this video):** earthy olive green, muted ochre,
dusky maroon, and deep indigo-violet — a muted, slightly
psychedelic-but-restrained documentary palette matching the mushroom/ritual
subject matter, appended to the `diagram` suffix only (matching video 001's
own precedent — `character`/`environment` suffixes are used verbatim,
unmodified, per `system/04_shots/shot.md`).

**Generation-ready exports** (regenerate both from this file if it changes,
